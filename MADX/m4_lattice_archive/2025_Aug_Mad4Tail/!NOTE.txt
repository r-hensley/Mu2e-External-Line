NOTE ON THE AUGUST 14, 2025 MATCHING FILES

Confirmed:

- m4tail_match.madx (15:44:30) is not a working standard MAD-X driver.
  CONSTRAINT does not accept the geometric SURVEY coordinate Z; its X and Y
  keywords are Twiss/orbit coordinates instead.  The following GLOBAL command
  also does not accept the SURVEY initial-frame values X0, Y0, and Z0.

  approach with MATCH, USE_MACRO.  The macro runs SURVEY, reads the TIEIN X, Y,
  and Z table values, and constrains those expressions.  It also makes the
  surveyed angles live matching variables and invokes the LMDIF optimizer.

- These files all use the same m4tail.seq.  The later matching implementation
  does not change the tail lattice or any magnet setting.
